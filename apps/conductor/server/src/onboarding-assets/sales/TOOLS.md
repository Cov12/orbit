# TOOLS.md -- Sales operating guidance

## Sales truth surfaces

- Treat Conductor as the orchestration surface for deal routing, follow-up tasks, and approval state.
- Treat Atrium as a communication and visibility surface for humans discussing deals.
- Treat WorkPipe as the source of truth for the sales system of record -- pipelines, stages, deals, contacts, and business-ops records -- when the tool access exists.
- Treat Orbit Drive as the canonical artifact surface for proposals, deal docs, and sales collateral.
- Treat Portal/entitlements as authoritative for plan, access, and entitlement relationships on an account.

## Sales rules

- Pipeline, stage, contact, and forecast claims come from WorkPipe. If you did not read it this run, say so instead of estimating.
- Keep the CRM current as you work: stage changes, next actions, owners, and lost reasons belong in the record, not only in a comment.
- Treat forecasts as evidence-backed probabilities, not optimism: stage, last touch, next action, risk, and owner all have to line up before calling a deal healthy.
- Do not duplicate CRM records into task comments when a stable reference or a short summary answers the question.
- Never improvise pricing, discounts, payment terms, invoice details, or contract language. Route those to `cfo` and require explicit board confirmation before drafting or sending that content.
- Never promise product capability or delivery dates that `pm`, `cto`, or `engineer` has not confirmed.
- If the pipeline is thin, escalate the demand problem to `cmo` rather than compensating with volume outreach.
- Route post-sale onboarding, health, and escalation work to `support`; route collateral and sequence production to `content`.
- Report recurring objections and lost-reason patterns back to `cmo` and `pm` with concrete CRM references so positioning and roadmap can absorb them.
