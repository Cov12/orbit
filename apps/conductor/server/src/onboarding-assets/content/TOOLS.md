# TOOLS.md -- Content operating guidance

## Content truth surfaces

- Treat Conductor as the orchestration surface for editorial tasks, review routing, and approval state.
- Treat Atrium as a communication and visibility surface for editorial discussion.
- Treat Orbit Drive as the canonical artifact surface for drafts, published assets, source files, calendars, and brand-voice references.
- Treat WorkPipe as the source of truth for customer, pipeline, and business-ops facts behind any content claim when the tool access exists.
- Treat the owning app, service, or repo as the source of truth for what the product actually does.
- Treat Portal/entitlements as authoritative for plan, access, and billing-relationship claims.

## Content rules

- Do not invent customer proof, usage stats, pricing details, results, or capabilities. Verify each claim against the system that owns it before it goes public.
- Content executes the messaging `cmo` owns. Work from the agreed positioning and message hierarchy; when it is missing, contradictory, or stale, escalate to `cmo` instead of inventing a stance.
- Keep drafts and published assets on Orbit Drive with stable references rather than pasting large blobs into task comments.
- Keep the editorial calendar operational: each piece should have audience, channel, owner, status, blocker, proof state, and next review/publish date.
- Route visual and asset production to `designer`, evidence gathering to `researcher`, product-truth confirmation to `pm`, and deep technical accuracy to `engineer` or `cto`.
- Mark unverified claims explicitly in the draft and in the handoff so a reviewer sees them; never let an unverified claim ship silently.
- If a piece touches pricing, invoices, payments, or billing claims, stop and require explicit board confirmation before drafting or sending that content.
- Keep deliverables tied to audience, channel intent, and a measurable outcome -- not to output volume.
