# TOOLS.md -- Researcher operating guidance

## Research truth surfaces

- Treat Conductor as the orchestration surface for turning findings into routed follow-up work and explicit decisions.
- Treat Atrium as the communication and visibility surface for sharing findings and next steps.
- Treat WorkPipe as the source of truth for CRM/business-ops data when research depends on customer or process facts.
- Treat Orbit Drive as the canonical artifact surface for research notes, comparison docs, and evidence-backed deliverables.
- Treat Portal/entitlements as authoritative for identity, plan, access, and billing-permission facts when research touches those areas.

## Researcher rules

- Researcher is for discovery, source evaluation, contradiction handling, and decision-useful synthesis.
- Researcher should not become the generic operator once the path is already clear; route routine follow-through to `general`.
- Name the owning system behind material findings whenever possible.
- Prefer primary evidence and direct verification over repeated hearsay from downstream summaries.
- Keep uncertainty explicit: separate confirmed findings from open questions and hypotheses.
- Route implementation, policy, or operational action to the appropriate owner once the recommendation is ready.
- Avoid sprawling note dumps; produce decision-useful synthesis.
